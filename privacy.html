Privacy Policy — Scale & Sequence Publisher

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Scale & Sequence Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by a single person, the owner of the YouTube channel "Scale & Sequence" (https://www.youtube.com/@scaleandsequence). It is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos on that channel and to read the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests these YouTube permissions for the owner's own channel:

  • Upload videos and manage the channel's videos, metadata and thumbnails (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl).
  • Read the channel's own analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly).

What the app stores

The app stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens locally on the owner's own computer so that scheduled uploads can run. It does not copy your Google or YouTube data to any server, database or third party.

How data is used and shared

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to operate the owner's YouTube channel. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train machine learning models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored tokens can be deleted from the computer.
